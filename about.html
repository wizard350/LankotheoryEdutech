Who We Are

Education With Purpose

LANKOTHEORY Educational Services is an academic learning and preparation platform focused on helping students prepare for important examinations and educational milestones.

Our programs cover areas such as JAMB, IJMB, JUPEB, WAEC, NECO, BECE, Post-UTME and other academic preparation programs.

We believe that effective education is more than memorizing information. Students need clear explanations, quality learning resources, consistent practice and the right academic guidance.
